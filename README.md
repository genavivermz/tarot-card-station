# Tarot
## /ˈterō/
- A practice using a specialized deck of cards as a visual and symbolic tool for introspection and self-discovery.

## Instructions
- Say your question out loud while shuffling
- Tap on card deck and keep pulling to reveal a new card
- Click on a card to read its meaning
- When you're ready, re-shuffle the deck and ask something else!

### Happy introspecting!
